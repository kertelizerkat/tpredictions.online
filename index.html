System Limitations

While advanced, our prediction system has inherent constraints

Unpredictable Events

The system cannot account for sudden changes like player injuries or unexpected tactical shifts.

Data Quality Dependencies

Predictions are only as good as the data they're based on. Incomplete or outdated data can lead to less accurate predictions.

Human Element

Factors like team chemistry, player motivation, or coaching strategies are difficult to quantify and may not be fully captured.

Environmental Factors

While some external factors are considered, sudden changes in weather or pitch conditions can still impact the outcome.

Data Privacy Constraints

To comply with regulations, certain data might not be collected or used, potentially limiting the model's accuracy.

Statistical Probabilities

Even with advanced algorithms, football matches can be unpredictable, and there's always a margin of error in predictions.
